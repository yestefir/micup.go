### F. Regression & Risk Assessment

The result channel acts as the existing reservation's ownership identity, without adding a public generation field. Commit and cleanup compare it while holding the same mutex used by reset. Reset may release a guild before an earlier caller finishes cleanup; the identity guard is therefore required to protect replacement joins. Result queues are drained/replaced under that mutex, following the earlier terminal-notification pattern. Terminal shutdown continues to retain reservations until caller cleanup, and resumable reconnects do not execute reset invalidation.

Reset notification does not interrupt a JoinVoice still inside Send; it observes the result after that operation returns. Successful grant commits before a reset retain their normal linear ordering with the subsequent reset/voice-loss callback. This change does not tag server events with epochs, prove every concurrent unsolicited rotation/terminal-shutdown interleaving, or change active socket write cancellation. No new regression required a revert.
