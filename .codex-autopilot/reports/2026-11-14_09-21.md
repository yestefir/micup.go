### B. Discovered Issues

**P2 — Upload metadata/URLs can fail after file side effects or yield unusable success.** `UploadAttachment` did not require an upload key or multipart ID and checked individual part URLs only after reading their file segments. Missing keys/IDs returned nil errors under permissive fixtures; invalid later URLs consumed all six test bytes and uploaded the first part before returning an error.

**P2 — URL userinfo causes upload Authorization.** `put` allowed a username/password in the returned URL. The local HTTP receiver observed a nonempty Authorization header in both singlepart and multipart cases, even though the client sets no bot Authorization for uploads. These were synthetic URL-supplied Basic credentials, not a demonstrated bot-token leak. The [upstream upload guide](https://docs.fluxer.com/topics/uploads/) describes data PUTs without Authorization and completion using the upload key/ID; the new checks align with that transport/return contract and the existing REST-base URL rejection of userinfo.
